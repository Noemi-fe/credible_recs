# Credible picks

Three picks for skincare and kitchen gear, ranked by how credible the people recommending them are on Reddit, not by
how popular a product is. Every quote is checked word for word against its comment, and links back to it.

A free, non-commercial research prototype. The code is at https://github.com/Noemi-fe/credible_recs; this Space only
builds it (the Dockerfile) and starts it. "How we pick and score", in the app, explains how answers are made and what
is collected about visitors.
